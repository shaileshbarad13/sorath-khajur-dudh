Why Khajur Dudh in Winter?

શિયાળામાં ખજૂર દૂધ કેમ?

🔥

Warm and comforting

ગરમાગરમ મોજ

A hot cup of milk and dates is a classic way to beat the winter chill.

⚡

Natural energy

કુદરતી શક્તિ

Dates carry natural sugars with fibre, so they give a steady lift without refined sugar.

🥛

Calcium and protein

કેલ્શિયમ અને પ્રોટીન

Milk adds calcium and protein that help keep bones and muscles strong.

🍃

Fibre and minerals

ફાઇબર અને ખનિજ

Dates contain fibre, potassium, magnesium and a little iron.

Sip it warm on a cold evening
ઠંડી સાંજે ગરમ પીવો

Warm milk is soothing, and the dates make it naturally sweet.

Dates are naturally high in sugar, so enjoy in moderation. If you have diabetes or lactose intolerance, check with your doctor first. This is general information, not medical advice.
