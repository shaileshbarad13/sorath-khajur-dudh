Why Khajur Dudh in Winter?

શિયાળામાં ખજૂર દૂધ કેમ?

🔥

Warm and comforting

ગરમાગરમ મોજ

A hot cup of milk and dates is a classic way to beat the winter chill.

⚡

Natural energy

કુદરતી શક્તિ

Dates carry natural sugars with fibre, so they give a steady lift.

🥛

Calcium and protein

કેલ્શિયમ અને પ્રોટીન

Milk adds calcium and protein that help keep bones and muscles strong.

🍃

Fibre and minerals

ફાઇબર અને ખનિજ

Dates contain fibre, potassium, magnesium and a little iron.

🌿

Good for digestion

પાચન માટે સારું

The fibre in dates supports regular digestion.

😋

Keeps you full

પેટ ભરેલું રાખે

Milk protein plus date fibre makes it a filling late-night treat.

🍯

Natural sweetness

કુદરતી મીઠાશ

Dates sweeten the drink on their own, so no refined sugar is needed.

🛡️

Rich in antioxidants

એન્ટીઑક્સિડન્ટથી ભરપૂર

Dates contain plant antioxidants such as flavonoids and carotenoids.

💪

Great after a workout

કસરત પછી ઉત્તમ

Protein and quick carbs from milk and dates help you recover.

Sip it warm on a cold evening
ઠંડી સાંજે ગરમ પીવો

Warm milk is soothing, and the dates make it naturally sweet.

Dates are naturally high in sugar, so enjoy in moderation. If you have diabetes or lactose intolerance, check with your doctor first. This is general information, not medical advice.
